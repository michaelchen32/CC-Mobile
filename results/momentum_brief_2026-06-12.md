2. **Financials are leading and tech is following.** The AI mega-caps that drove May are *absent or dropped*; the leadership baton has clearly passed to banks/insurers/asset-managers + cyclical materials (steel). That rotation in *leadership* (vs. the day-to-day name churn) is the more durable signal.

3. **Mind the euphoria.** "The Index Says Euphoria" + a 27%-of-universe breakout + parabolic 1-yr moves (NUE +124%) are classic late-stage-thrust markers. Breadth thrusts can be powerful *continuation* signals OR exhaustion tops; this screen can't distinguish those — it only tells you participation is extreme right now.
