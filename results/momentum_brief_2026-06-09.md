1. **The semi bounce is structurally weak.** All 9 fired on gap_go alone (n=1). These are one-day gaps, not confirmed multi-detector base breakouts. SMCI in particular gapped then *slid on a $7B dilution raise* — its signal is the lowest quality in the set.

This is the one cluster where the technical signal and the fundamental catalyst (Medicaid contract wins, diabetes-device data) actually align.
